JUPITER

The Giant Planet

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. Known for its enormous size, powerful storms and many moons, it is one of the most fascinating planets in our cosmic neighbourhood.

Jupiter at a Glance

  • Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system.
  • Jupiter has a very strong magnetic field.
  • Jupiter has 79 known moons.
  • Jupiter is mostly made of gas and liquid.
  1. Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun.
  2. Jupiter has a very short day, lasting about 10 hours.
  3. Jupiter has a very long year, lasting about 12 Earth years.
